Who are we?

At Vinea Care, our vision is creating a better everyday life for all the people we serve. Our mission is delivering quality care compassionately. Vinea, which is latin for Vineyard, is where we grew up calling home and learnt the values we now take to others. These values are at the core of our services.

Our well-trained care takers assist with everyday household, personal, social, and medical tasks. We ensure that your life is in good order and that you can enjoy your time with us in comfort, peace, and joy. Our services include:

  • Live-in care
  • Child Care
  • Visiting Care
  • Specialised Care

Our Services

At Vinea Care we understand that every individual is different and has specific needs, hence we appoint each of our clients to our experienced coordinators to ensure the care plan you receive will meet all your needs. We encourage dialogue with our clients and enjoy your reviews on our services. Our dedicated team have a 24/7 line to ensure you receive the support you need. Get in touch with us to find out more.

Live-in care

A fully trained carer will be living with you and will support you with your daily needs, keeping you comfortable and independent from the comfort of your own home.

Child Care

Our team of dedicated caregivers are here to support you with your childcare needs. Whether you need someone to look after your children while you're at work, or you need someone to help with school runs and other childcare needs, we're here to help.

Respite Care

Have you been caring for a loved one and are now looking for a temporary helping hand? Vinea care has a team of dedicated caregivers who can give you the helping hand you need.

Visiting Care

Let us arrange regular visits where our caregivers can handle tasks such as laundry, house cleaning, meal preparation, and running errands.
